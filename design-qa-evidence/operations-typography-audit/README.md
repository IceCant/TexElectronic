# Operations typography audit

Scope: POS and shared internal operations typography across dashboard, inventory, products, locations, forms, staff workflows, and mobile layouts.

## Flow

1. **POS before — poor readability.** Product subtitles, SKU/bin codes, stock counts, helper text, totals, and status labels used 7–9px text.
2. **POS catalogue after — healthy.** Product titles are 18px; subtitles use 13px; SKU/location text uses 12px; prices use 17px; categories and helper text use 12–15px.
3. **POS cart after — healthy.** Cart identity, quantity controls, totals, and payment actions are larger. Quantity and remove controls also have explicit accessible names.
4. **Dashboard after — healthy.** Metrics, panel titles, activity descriptions, dates, and empty states use a readable operational hierarchy.
5. **Inventory after — healthy.** Summary labels, tabs, filters, table headings, rows, locations, and statuses are readable without removing useful density.
6. **Mobile staff after — healthy.** Action names use 15px text and descriptions use 11px text with clean two-column reflow.
7. **Mobile POS after — healthy.** Product identities and technical text remain readable at 390px, and the horizontal category scrollbar is visually hidden.

## Evidence limits

The captured screens verify visible hierarchy, common responsive reflow, and accessible names exposed in the DOM. They do not constitute full WCAG conformance or testing across every browser zoom and OS text-scaling preference.
