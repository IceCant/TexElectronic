# Comfortable storefront type audit

Scope: second typography pass requested after the initial increase still felt compact. Checked on the public catalogue, cart, and order tracking in desktop and 390 × 844 mobile layouts.

## Flow

1. **Storefront before second pass — needs improvement.** The earlier four-column layout still made supporting text and catalogue content feel visually compressed.
2. **Desktop storefront after — healthy.** Navigation, hero copy, trust details, search, and categories now use an obvious retail-scale hierarchy.
3. **Desktop product cards after — healthy.** The catalogue uses three wider columns with 20px product names, 13px metadata, 14px descriptions, and larger prices and actions.
4. **Mobile storefront after — healthy.** Larger text reflows without page-level horizontal overflow; primary actions stack to avoid cramped wrapping.
5. **Mobile cart after — healthy.** Product names, quantities, prices, and removal actions remain readable and clearly grouped.
6. **Mobile tracking after — healthy.** Instructions, labels, inputs, privacy guidance, and status content use the same comfortable scale.

## Evidence limits

Screenshots confirm visible hierarchy and responsive reflow at the captured sizes. This does not replace testing with every browser zoom level, OS text-scaling preference, or assistive technology.
